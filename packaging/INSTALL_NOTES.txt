FRAME 0.5.4 Beta

FRAME keeps the last minutes of your screen (Shadow Recording) so you can save a clip with a hotkey after something happens, records manually, and has a Library with trimming and "Crop for social".

Before you install:

- Installs for your Windows account only (no administrator rights needed), into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\FRAME.
- FRAME needs FFmpeg 8.1 or newer, a free video tool that is installed separately. If it is missing, FRAME explains how to install it the first time it starts (one command, shown in a window you control).
- Your settings and logs are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FRAME and your recordings in your Videos\FRAME folder (or the folder you choose). Installing, upgrading or uninstalling FRAME never deletes your recordings.
- FRAME sends nothing anywhere: no telemetry, no automatic crash reports. If something goes wrong you can export a diagnostics ZIP yourself (Settings > Advanced > Export diagnostics) and decide whether to share it.
- This beta is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn before it starts ("More info" > "Run anyway").

If FRAME is running, quit it first (right-click the tray icon > Quit FRAME).
